The figures in this guide are the ones a supplier should be held to in writing. For a 10 liter PET blow molding machine in PET bottle manufacturers production, they cover rate, tolerance, materials, control, changeover and acceptance.

What This Equipment Does

Capacity in bottles per hour is only one figure. The 10 liter PET blow molding machine also has to hold 2.0 % weight tolerance, run 16 oz format and cycle within the 15-minute changeover window.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference 10 liter PET blow molding machine uses 304/316L stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 42–57 kW connected load and a 1800 × 1900 × 2400 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 1800 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a 10 liter PET blow molding machine arrives on a PET bottle manufacturers floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the 10 liter PET blow molding machine over five years rather than one budget cycle. Moulds, contact parts in 304/316L stainless steel and the 2.0 % tolerance all have different cost curves over that horizon.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this 10 liter PET blow molding machine the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 15-minute routine service realistic on a 16166 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the 10 liter PET blow molding machine run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The PET bottle manufacturers team still owns the routine checks, but the 15-minute changeover is guided.

How does the 10 liter PET blow molding machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the 10 liter PET blow molding machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

Can the 10 liter PET blow molding machine handle a 16 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 15-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
