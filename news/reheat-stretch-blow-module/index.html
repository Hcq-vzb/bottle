Reheat Stretch Blow Module

Published 2026-10-27 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a reheat stretch blow module for PET bottle manufacturers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

A reheat stretch blow module turns molten polymer into a hollow container, and the three things it must control are the parison, the mould and the cooling. This PET bottle manufacturers build produces 15483–19983 bottles per hour for 2 L format.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification to hold a reheat stretch blow module to is: 15483–19983 bottles per hour, 0.25–1.25 L, 1.5 % tolerance, SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts, 8.5–11.5 kW and a 3200 × 2800 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a reheat stretch blow module is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the PET bottle manufacturers production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the reheat stretch blow module against the resin cost of the bottles it rejects. At 1.5 % weight tolerance, the scrap saved on 15483 bottles per minute usually outweighs the machine cost inside the first year.

Maintenance and Service

Choose a supplier that holds spare parts locally and answers support requests in writing. Preventive work on the reheat stretch blow module concentrates on the seals, sensors and drives — wear items rather than failure items.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this reheat stretch blow module hold in real production?

The reference build holds 1.5 ml on 2 L product at 15483–19983 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How long does a format changeover take on this reheat stretch blow module?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 15 minutes. The first change to a new 2 L format takes longer.

What is the actual connected load of the reheat stretch blow module?

The reference build draws 8.5–11.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
