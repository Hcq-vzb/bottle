Two-Cavity Blow Molder

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machine

There is a short answer and a long answer to specifying a two-cavity blow molder. The short one is the rate; the long one — and the one that determines whether the PET bottle manufacturers production line runs — is everything the rate depends on.

What This Equipment Does

A two-cavity blow molder occupies one defined station in the production line for PET bottle manufacturers. This build runs at 4294–5794 units per hour for 20 L product, and the configuration at that rate determines output consistency, labour and finished quality.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare a two-cavity blow molder on weight tolerance and on layer capability, not on bottles per hour alone. This build holds 2.0 % on 20 L format and runs 304/316L stainless steel on the product-contact surfaces.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the two-cavity blow molder determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 4294–5794 units per hour and a 20-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the two-cavity blow molder over five years rather than one budget cycle. Moulds, contact parts in 304/316L stainless steel and the 2.0 % tolerance all have different cost curves over that horizon.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this two-cavity blow molder the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 20-minute routine service realistic on a 4294 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the two-cavity blow molder handle a 20 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 20-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Can the two-cavity blow molder be upgraded later?

Within the 4294–5794 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 20 L range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
