Rotary Blow Molding Machine

Published 2026-11-06 · KIWL Machine

A rotary blow molding machine for PET bottle manufacturers is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what a rotary blow molding machine removes from the PET bottle manufacturers process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 16 oz product at 2284–3284 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This rotary blow molding machine draws 23–34 kW across a 1800 × 2800 × 2800 mm footprint with 304 stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 2.0 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 2284–3284 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the rotary blow molding machine to real 16 oz volume, then let the 2.0 ml tolerance and the 2284–3284 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Remote support matters for a rotary blow molding machine, because the fastest diagnosis comes from the 7-inch colour touch screen data: the last 20 minutes of weight trend usually identify a parison problem on its own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the rotary blow molding machine handle a 16 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 20-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 7-inch colour touch.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for PET bottle manufacturers operations running audits.

How is the rotary blow molding machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
