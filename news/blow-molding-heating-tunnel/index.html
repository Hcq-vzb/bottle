Blow Molding Heating Tunnel

Published 2026-11-02 · KIWL Machine

Where a blow molding heating tunnel specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the PET bottle manufacturers requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

Capacity in bottles per hour is only one figure. The blow molding heating tunnel also has to hold 0.5 % weight tolerance, run 12 oz format and cycle within the 8-minute changeover window.

Key Technical Specifications

A blow molding heating tunnel is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±0.5 ml, 6377–7877 units per hour, 3–4 kW, and 3200 × 3600 × 3200 mm, on 12 oz product.

Integration With Your Line

Plan upstream buffer, downstream transfer and shared CIP or control logic so the blow molding heating tunnel does not become the bottleneck. On this build the machine accepts a standard encoder interface and a 3200 mm working width, making it straightforward to index against existing PET bottle manufacturers conveyors.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the blow molding heating tunnel on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with 316L stainless steel contact parts and a 0.5 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same PET bottle manufacturers production line.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the blow molding heating tunnel. After a few months the pattern of the mould seal tells a PET bottle manufacturers operation the real service interval.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this blow molding heating tunnel hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.5 ml on 12 oz product at 6377–7877 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How does the blow molding heating tunnel behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the blow molding heating tunnel determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

What spare parts should be held for the blow molding heating tunnel?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 6377 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
