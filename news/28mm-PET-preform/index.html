28mm PET Preform

Published 2026-11-13 · KIWL Machine

Most disagreements about a 28mm PET preform come down to what was never written down. This guide sets out the PET bottle manufacturers requirements that decide whether a machine works: what it must deliver at 15749–20249 units per hour, what it is built from, and what to verify before handover.

What This Equipment Does

A 28mm PET preform should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 250–500 ml product at 15749–20249 units per hour, which is the span most PET bottle manufacturers production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for a 28mm PET preform contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 0.5 ml tolerance, the 316L stainless steel contact-part grade, the 1.5–2.5 kW load and the 1800 × 1900 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A 28mm PET preform that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 1800 mm working width, which is what lets the PET bottle manufacturers production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The business case for a 28mm PET preform is arithmetic before it is technical: 0.5 ml per unit, 15749–20249 units per hour, and a shift count that adds up across a year. Work it that way and the answer is usually within 12–36 months at regional PET bottle manufacturers scale.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the 28mm PET preform. After a few months the pattern of the seals and the drive tells you the real service interval, which is usually shorter than the manual claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 15749–20249 kN.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
