Bottle Leak Tester

Published 2026-10-25 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a bottle leak tester for PET bottle manufacturers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The unit a bottle leak tester performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 1 L product and delivers 14735–18735 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 14735–18735 units per hour means little without 1 L product, a 2.0 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover — all three stated for this bottle leak tester.

Integration With Your Line

A bottle leak tester fits an existing PET bottle manufacturers production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 3200 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the bottle leak tester to real 1 L volume, then let the 2.0 ml tolerance and the 14735–18735 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Choose a supplier that holds spare parts locally and answers support requests in writing. Preventive work on the bottle leak tester concentrates on the seals, sensors and drives — wear items rather than failure items.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the bottle leak tester handle a 1 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 8-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

What is the actual connected load of the bottle leak tester?

The reference build draws 13–18 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How does the bottle leak tester behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the bottle leak tester determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
