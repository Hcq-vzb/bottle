48mm PET Preform

Published 2026-11-26 · KIWL Machine

Most disagreements about a 48mm pet preform come down to what was never written down. This guide sets out the PET bottle manufacturers requirements that decide whether a machine works: what it must deliver at 9604–13104 units per hour, what it is built from, and what to verify before handover.

What This Equipment Does

Ask a PET bottle manufacturers plant engineer to describe their best-performing 48mm pet preform and they will describe the one that runs for weeks without intervention. This configuration is specified at 9604–13104 units per hour on 250–500 ml product with that as the design target.

Key Technical Specifications

The three figures that move the price most are accuracy, contact-part grade and the control platform. On this 48mm pet preform the tolerance is 0.3 ml, the contact parts are SUS304 with sanitary fittings, and the controls run on a 4-inch HMI.

Integration With Your Line

Plan upstream buffer, downstream transfer and shared CIP or control logic so the 48mm pet preform does not become the bottleneck. On this build the machine accepts a standard encoder interface and a 2400 mm working width, making it straightforward to index against existing PET bottle manufacturers conveyors.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a 48mm pet preform is downtime avoided during a 12-minute format change on a 250–500 ml format, because the PET bottle manufacturers production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this 48mm pet preform are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What clamping force do I need?

It follows the projected area of the part and the resin's flow rate; the reference build covers 9604–13104 kN.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

Can the machine run different moulds?

Yes, the machine is set up for tool changeover; the parameter backup is stored per tool.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
