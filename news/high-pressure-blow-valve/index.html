High Pressure Blow Valve

Published 2026-11-04 · KIWL Machine

A high pressure blow valve sits on the critical path of the PET bottle manufacturers production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a high pressure blow valve.

What This Equipment Does

Every machine on a line should have one sentence that explains why it exists. For this high pressure blow valve it is: it processes 500 ml product at 6104–7604 units per hour, consistently, without the PET bottle manufacturers team chasing it around.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This high pressure blow valve draws 14–19 kW across a 1800 × 1900 × 2400 mm footprint with food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The high pressure blow valve is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the PET bottle manufacturers workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A high pressure blow valve with a 4-inch HMI and a 15-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a PET bottle manufacturers buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this high pressure blow valve are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the high pressure blow valve run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 4-inch HMI, yes. The PET bottle manufacturers team still owns the routine checks, but the 15-minute changeover is guided.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How long does a format changeover take on this high pressure blow valve?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 15 minutes. The first change to a new 500 ml format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
