Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the high speed reheat stretch blow machine?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1605 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

What does the high speed reheat stretch blow machine do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

What is the delivery lead time for the high speed reheat stretch blow machine?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 2 L set is included.

How do I size this for my plant?

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?
