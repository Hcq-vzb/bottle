PET Stretch Blow Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-21 · KIWL Machine

A PET stretch blow molding machine for PET bottle manufacturers is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

For a PET bottle manufacturers producer the useful question about a PET stretch blow molding machine is how it behaves with a resin batch change, because the parison programming has to absorb density differences.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a PET stretch blow molding machine specification for what is measurable: rate 3600–5100 units per hour, deviation ±0.3 ml, container 500 ml, control 7-inch colour touch, load 35–50 kW, footprint 1600 × 1400 × 2100 mm, changeover 8 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the PET stretch blow molding machine determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 3600–5100 units per hour and a 8-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the PET stretch blow molding machine to real 500 ml volume, then let the 0.3 ml tolerance and the 3600–5100 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the PET stretch blow molding machine. After a few months the pattern of the seals and the drive tells you the real service interval, which is usually shorter than the manual claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery lead time for the PET stretch blow molding machine?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 500 ml set is included.

Does the PET stretch blow molding machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1400 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

Is the PET stretch blow molding machine suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 3600–5100 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
