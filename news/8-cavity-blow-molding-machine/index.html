Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 8 cavity blow molding machine suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 1352–2002 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

What is the actual connected load of the 8 cavity blow molding machine?

The reference build draws 14–19 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

Is a food-grade 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 2 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
