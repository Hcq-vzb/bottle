Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 5 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

Can the blow molding machine for 5 gallon bottles handle a 5 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 8-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 7-inch colour touch.

What spare parts should be held for the blow molding machine for 5 gallon bottles?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 8782 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?
