Servo Blow Molding Machine

Published 2026-11-15 · KIWL Machine

Before any servo blow molding machine quotation is compared, the rate has to be tied to a product and a format. This guide does that for PET bottle manufacturers operations and then works through the rest of the decision.

What This Equipment Does

Ask a PET bottle manufacturers plant engineer to describe their best-performing servo blow molding machine and they will describe the one that runs for weeks without intervention. This configuration is specified at 15199–19199 units per hour on 20 L product with that as the design target.

Key Technical Specifications

A servo blow molding machine is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 15199–19199 units per hour, contact parts 304 stainless steel, control 10-inch colour touch, load 5.5–7.5 kW, footprint 2400 × 1900 × 3200 mm, changeover 20 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the PET bottle manufacturers team will adopt the routine — and a servo blow molding machine with a 20-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A servo blow molding machine rated at 15199–19199 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

Blow moulding wears at the mould, the die and the seals. Preventive work on the servo blow molding machine concentrates on those, and on this build the mould seal and the drive are the items worth stocking for a PET bottle manufacturers operation running 15199 bottles per minute.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the servo blow molding machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the servo blow molding machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

What spare parts should be held for the servo blow molding machine?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 15199 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How long does a format changeover take on this servo blow molding machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 20 minutes. The first change to a new 20 L format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
