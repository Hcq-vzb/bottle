6 Cavity Blow Molding Machine

Published 2026-10-30 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a 6 cavity blow molding machine for PET bottle manufacturers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a 6 cavity blow molding machine is not the demonstration but the third month. At 10459–13959 units per hour for 60–120 mm diameter product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a 6 cavity blow molding machine specification for what is measurable: rate 10459–13959 units per hour, deviation ±2.0 ml, container 60–120 mm diameter, control 7-inch colour touch, load 5.5–7.5 kW, footprint 2400 × 1400 × 2100 mm, changeover 12 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 2400 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a 6 cavity blow molding machine arrives on a PET bottle manufacturers floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Three mechanisms produce the return on a 6 cavity blow molding machine: more saleable bottles per hour, less scrap and shorter format changes. At 10459–13959 bottles per hour all three move together for a PET bottle manufacturers operation.

Maintenance and Service

For a PET bottle manufacturers operation the practical measure of after-sales support is how quickly the 6 cavity blow molding machine returns to 10459 bottles per minute after a fault, and that is decided by parts availability rather than by the size of the service team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the 6 cavity blow molding machine handle a 60–120 mm diameter product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 12-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 7-inch colour touch.

How is the 6 cavity blow molding machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Can the 6 cavity blow molding machine be upgraded later?

Within the 10459–13959 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 60–120 mm diameter range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
