PET Jar Blowing Machine

Published 2026-11-19 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a PET jar blowing machine for PET bottle manufacturers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The unit a PET jar blowing machine performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 2 L product and delivers 2714–3714 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

A PET jar blowing machine is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±0.5 ml, 2714–3714 units per hour, 3–4 kW, and 2400 × 3600 × 2800 mm, on 2 L product.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This PET jar blowing machine takes a standard encoder signal and a 2400 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the PET jar blowing machine on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with 304/316L stainless steel contact parts and a 0.5 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same PET bottle manufacturers production line.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive discipline on a PET jar blowing machine is easier when the intervals are stated in cycles rather than months. At 2714–3714 units per hour, this build's seals and sensors reach end of life far sooner than a quarterly check would suggest.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the PET jar blowing machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the PET jar blowing machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How is the PET jar blowing machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Does the PET jar blowing machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 3600 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
