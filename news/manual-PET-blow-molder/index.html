Manual PET Blow Molder

Published 2026-11-16 · KIWL Machine

Choosing the right manual PET blow molder is a practical decision for PET bottle manufacturers. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting a manual PET blow molder on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

What a manual PET blow molder does to the PET bottle manufacturers production line is measurable at the packing end. At 9339–12839 units per hour for 500 ml can product, the shift output stops depending on how the previous hour went.

Key Technical Specifications

Where two manual PET blow molder quotations look identical, the difference is in the contact parts and the control platform. This reference build specifies SUS304 with sanitary fittings surfaces, a 7-inch colour touch, a 3.0 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover across 500 ml can format.

Integration With Your Line

A blow moulding line is only as good as the handshake between the extruder and the mould. This manual PET blow molder accepts the standard encoder interface across a 1200 mm working width, so the parison and the mould stay in phase.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A manual PET blow molder rated at 9339–12839 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

The manual PET blow molder was designed so the routine work happens during a changeover rather than during production. Seals, sensors and drives are grouped for access, which is what allows a 9339 unit per hour PET bottle manufacturers production line to maintain without a shutdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

What does the manual PET blow molder do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

Can the manual PET blow molder run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The PET bottle manufacturers team still owns the routine checks, but the 8-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
