Blow Molding Air Compressor

Published 2026-11-03 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a blow molding air compressor for PET bottle manufacturers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The blow molding air compressor is where polymer becomes a package. At 6231–9231 bottles per hour for 2 L format, the wall consistency it holds is the whole argument.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification to hold a blow molding air compressor to is: 6231–9231 bottles per hour, 0.25–1.25 L, 3.0 % tolerance, 304 stainless steel contact parts, 6.5–8.5 kW and a 3200 × 2800 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 3200 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a blow molding air compressor arrives on a PET bottle manufacturers floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A blow molding air compressor with a 4-inch HMI and a 8-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a PET bottle manufacturers buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Put the blow molding air compressor on a preventive schedule that counts cycles rather than calendar months. At 6231–9231 units per hour the seals reach end of life long before a quarterly inspection notices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the blow molding air compressor need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 2800 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

What does the blow molding air compressor do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How long does a format changeover take on this blow molding air compressor?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 2 L format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
